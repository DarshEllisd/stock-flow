Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

StockFlow is a demonstration marketplace that connects buyers and sellers. This page explains what information StockFlow collects, how it is used and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name and email address, and confirmation that the email is verified. We request only the openid, email and profile scopes. We never see your Google password.
  • Information you add: an optional phone number, delivery or warehouse addresses, and for sellers, product listings, prices, stock levels and product images.
  • Activity in the app: your cart, orders, order approvals, notifications and wallet transactions.
  • Chatbot messages: what you type into the customer-service chatbot.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To run the marketplace: show listings, process orders, notify buyers and sellers, and keep wallet balances.
  • To answer chatbot questions. Your message, along with your name, email and a summary of your recent orders, cart, notifications (or, for sellers, your stock and pending approvals) and the product catalogue, is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Sellers see the buyer's name and order details, which can include the delivery address, for orders that include their products.
  • Buyers see the seller's name and email on product pages.
  • Product images are stored at public URLs so they can be displayed on product pages.

Where it is stored

StockFlow is hosted on Vercel. Account and order data is stored in a Neon Postgres database, and product images are stored in Vercel Blob storage. These services may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

Cookies

StockFlow uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in (a session cookie) and to protect forms (a CSRF cookie). Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Your choices

  • You can update your name and phone number on your profile page.
  • You can delete your account from the profile page. This removes your profile, addresses, cart, orders and notifications. Some records may be kept where they are needed to keep other users' order history intact.
  • You can revoke StockFlow's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: stock.flow.dbms@gmail.com.
